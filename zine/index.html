Your zine

Eight pages: cover, six posters, back. Tap a page or swipe to turn it.

Cover
Drag down to fold the sheet in half
  1. Fold the A4 sheet in half the long way, print on the outside.
  2. Cut along the fold, only through the middle two panels.
  3. Push the two ends toward each other so the cut opens into a plus.
  4. Swing the pages round and flatten them into a booklet, cover on top.
  5. Open it and turn the pages, right there on the table.
